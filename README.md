# Industrial Complexes in South Korea: Location and Tenants

> ### **[▶ Open the map](https://wherewindstay.github.io/korea-industrial-complexes/)**
>
> *The map is in Korean. GitHub does not run interactive content inside a README.*

An interactive map that stacks South Korea's industrial complexes (national, general, urban high-tech and agro-industrial) together with free trade zones, foreign investment zones, research and development special zones and knowledge industry centers, by year of designation or completion. Clicking a complex shows its sub-districts, quarterly operating rate, tenants, sale rate, employment, annual output and a list of tenant firms.

| | Count | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Industrial complexes | 1,370 (national 55 · general 774 · urban high-tech 55 · agro-industrial 486) | Industrial Location Information System, end of August 2026 |
| Free trade zones | 14 | Ministry of Trade designation notices |
| Foreign investment zones | 101 | Provincial notices, KOTRA guide |
| R&D special zones | 56 districts and zones | Ministry of Science and ICT notices |
| Knowledge industry centers | 1,553 | Factory On, end of September 2026 |

## Sources

- Complex list, designation and completion dates, development status and sub-districts: Korea Industrial Complex Corporation (KICOX), Industrial Location Information System
- Quarterly statistics: KICOX National Industrial Complex Statistics (KOSIS), 2001 Q1 – 2026 Q2
- Boundaries: Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport, industrial complex boundary map (data.go.kr 15152766)
- Tenant firms: KICOX Factory On tenant lists (end of September 2026; data.go.kr, no usage restriction), Korea Agro-Industrial Complex Association, INNOPOLIS Foundation tenant management system. Only firm name, products, employees and organisation type are shown; representative names and contact details are not included
- Free trade zones, foreign investment zones, R&D special zones: designation notices (National Law Information Center) and the managing agencies; sources are linked in each zone's detail panel
- Base map: © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL), tiles by OSM Deutschland

## Limitations

- Statistics before and after 2014 use different compilation bases and may break at the boundary
- Where Factory On lists several complexes under one name, tenant firms were not assigned; 86% of planned-location factories were matched to a complex
- The official list of individual-type foreign investment zones (July 2024) could not be obtained
- Knowledge industry centers have no approval or completion dates and are shown at their current locations; 1,087 of 1,553 could be located
- See the Korean README for the full methods and limitations
